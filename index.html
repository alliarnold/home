Alli Arnold (she/her) is a Brooklyn-based arts and culture expert with a community focus. Her experience spans arts administration, programming, research, community engagement, and strategic planning in the public and private sectors.
Alli has overseen the commissioning of new public artworks in numerous publicly owned buildings and mixed-use spaces throughout New York City, and she has served as a panelist for the New York Foundation for the Arts and the New York State Council on the Arts, assisting in reviewing grants to support artists and organizations throughout the state.
In her role at an arts and culture management consulting firm, Alli works with nonprofit arts organizations, local government agencies, universities, and various friends of the arts on arts planning projects throughout North America, collecting community feedback, designing surveys, conducting geo-demographic and arts infrastructure research, and business planning.
Alli holds an MS in Data Analysis and Visualization from the Graduate Center at CUNY, an MA in the Connoisseurship of Fine & Decorative Art from the University of Manchester’s Sotheby’s Institute of Art program, and a BA in Art History and French from Loyola University Chicago. And, she has worked in various programming, research, and curatorial capacities at cultural institutions around New York City, including A.I.R. Gallery, the Brooklyn Academy of Music, and the MTA’s Arts & Design.
As a data enthusiast, Alli has overseen the design and build-out of several custom art collection management databases, researched climate solutions, and created engaging visuals on topics ranging from audience engagement to aquaculture.
In her free time, Alli can be found knitting, reading cozy mysteries, and searching for the perfect cookie recipe.
